DRIVING

W throttle · S brake / reverse · A D steer.

SPACE handbrake. SHIFT boost. E get out.

Bicycles and e-scooters cut through the old town; taxis are quick, vans carry pace, and the TAG bus takes no prisoners.

Live traffic now shares the grid. Cars, taxis, vans and buses yield when you step in front of them.

Only parked vehicles with cyan map dots are drivable; ambient traffic keeps moving through town.

THE CITY

1 sunny · 2 rain · 3 snow. Weather is manual and instant.

M opens the live map — your exact position, heading, trams, lifts and trails.

Every metre travelled earns 1 reward point. Distance milestones pay bonuses and raise your explorer rank.

Five tram lines cross the city — A blue · B green · C pink · D orange · E purple. Walk to a platform for the live TAG board; E boards and exits only while the tram is stopped at a stop.

On board, the cabin display shows the next stop — PROCHAIN ARRÊT — with its live countdown; the mouse looks around the saloon.

The téléphérique leaves from the Isère bank and climbs to the Bastille.

THE MOUNTAINS

Find trailheads north of the river and southwest at Chamrousse. Follow the wooden signs.

In snow, the pistes above Chamrousse and the Bastille slopes become skiable.

Steep off-piste works too: point downhill, steer with A D, brake with SPACE.
